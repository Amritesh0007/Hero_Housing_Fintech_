Real estate decisions deserve better intelligence.

KSHETRA reads a flat the way a valuer does. It looks at what similar homes nearby are asking, what the same building is asking, and how size, floor and furnishing move the price. Then it tells you what it found.

Every estimate comes with a range, the factors that pushed it up or down, and the listings it was built from. If the evidence is thin, it says so instead of guessing.

What KSHETRA does

AI property valuation

A price estimate from a model trained on tens of thousands of Indian listings, checked against comparable homes before it reaches you.

Market intelligence

See how a locality’s asking price per square foot compares with the rest of the city, and where your flat sits within it.

Explainable insights

Each estimate breaks down into the factors behind it, in rupees, so you can see why the number is what it is.

How it works
